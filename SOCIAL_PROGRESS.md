# NoUs-Social — Content Ops Progress Log

This log tracks all content-ops activity for the NoUs project's social presence.

## Standing Rules
- Agents PREPARE and STAGE material only. Nothing is published without explicit human approval.
- Agents NEVER hold, create, or enter platform credentials/OAuth tokens. The human provisions all secrets.
- Work happens on branches + PRs. Never merge to main without owner sign-off.
- Archive, never delete. Pacing: small batches, no spam.
- Mission backbone: "safety through severance" — data rights, severance from Big Tech.
