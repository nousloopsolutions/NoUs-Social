# NoUs Loop

**safety through severance/**

NoUs Loop exists to sever big companies from your personal data. Your mind, your signals and your history stay on your machine, under your control, and the loop between you and your tools never routes through someone else's server.

## Manifesto

Every piece of NoUs work says one of these. If it says none of them, it isn't on brand.

1. **free your data/**: what is yours comes home: your history, your signals, your mind.
2. **severance/**: cut the line between you and the companies that harvest you.
3. **stop being a consumer tool/**: you are not the product, the user base, or the training set.
4. **protect yourself/**: local first, sovereign by default, nothing leaves without you.
5. **give them nothing/**: no data, no attention, no telemetry, no consent by default.
6. **take back from them. everything/**: not a toggle in their settings, a full exit.

On the wall these are sprayed lowercase in `tag-slogan`, each ending in the `neon-red` slash: the cut. In the product they become behaviour, not copy: offline by default, no tracking, export everything, delete everything.

## The name

**NoUs** is read two ways at once:

- **No Us**: no "us" in the middle. No company, no platform, no third party between a person and their own data.
- **Nous** (Greek): mind, intelligence. The thing we protect.

The **Loop** is spelled with the ∞ as its "oo": the mark is **NoUs L∞P**. In running text that can't carry the symbol, write "NoUs Loop", always with a capital N and U and a lowercase o and s. The mark is one connected machine in two acts. In the first act, its glyphs transform (NoUs → No Us → Nous → NoUS) while the ∞ tears and struggles, then lock back together. In the second, the sign falls dark and a rubber-hose Rube Goldberg chain reaction rebuilds it: one bump starts the next, each part lights as it's knocked home, until NoUs L∞P stands again. The ∞ is re-sprayed by a nozzle after every repair, then spins like a working turbine. The owl perches on it while the sign stands and flies off when it breaks.

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| NoUs L∞P (the mark) | NOUS LOOP, Nous loop, nousloop |
| NoUs Loop (plain text) | No-Us, NoUS |

## Slogans

Tags are written lowercase. The slash stays: it is the cut.

- **safety through severance/**: the primary tag. It goes on every wall, poster and cover.
- **let's sever ∞ humans**: campaign billboard line.
- **our sign of rebellion in an endless winter**: the caption beside the ∞ in the rebellion room.
- **No One Is Mean**: the founding double entendre. Socially, we don't treat people cruelly (no dark patterns). Statistically, we refuse to regress to the mean (we build for outliers).

## Voice

Two registers, never mixed in one sentence:

1. **The wall.** Short, lowercase, defiant, sprayed in the `tag` family. It is written in the imperative ("give them nothing", "take back everything"). It names the enemy (big tech, data brokers, surveillance) and never a person. Three to six words.
2. **The product.** Plain, calm, neurodivergent-friendly: plain language, no pressure, keyboard first. It says what happened, then what happens next. "Gateway unreachable. Retrying in 30 s." No exclamation marks, no blame, no urgency tricks.

Product labels are uppercase mono with wide tracking (`SEVERANCE LOG`, `DATA HELD LOCALLY`); sentences are sentence case.

**Every word is part of the revolution, placeholders included.** Example copy, placeholders, empty states, loading text and sample data all speak the cause; never "Lorem ipsum", "Click here" or "Loading…".

| Generic | NoUs |
| --- | --- |
| Loading… / Thinking… | severing… |
| Search… | which company has your data? |
| Submit / Run | Sever |
| Cancel | Not yet |
| Delete | Wipe their copy |
| Connected / Offline | Severed / Dark |
| No items yet | Nothing to sever yet. Add the first company that has your data. |
| Type a command | tell me what to take back… |

## Visual layers

The system has two layers that share one palette backbone.

### 1. Street: the brand wall

Used for: the brand book, campaign art, posters, covers, desktop wallpapers, landing heroes.

**The mood:** a near-future cyberpunk city where the corporations own the screens and the signal, and we are the revolutionaries tagging the walls. The work frees the mind from corporate greed. The look is electric blue on black: new, unfinished, sending a message, never polished.

**Steampunk meets cyberpunk:** our machines are honest machines: visible gears, sprockets, rivets, gauges and steam, but built of neon light, see-through, nothing hidden. The corporations hide their machinery; ours shows every joint.

**Clever winks (Escher and the infinite):** the design hides its ideals in impossible geometry.
- The ∞ crossing weaves over-under: an endless knot with no seam to cut.
- The **Emblem** is the surveillance pyramid upside down and woven into an impossible structure, with our lens where their eye was.
- The o in NoUs is a lens: the watched watch back.
- The name splits into "No Us" while the loop tears apart and the L and P lose power: severance costs something, and the loop always fights its way back.

- **Ground: a war-torn black wall.** A building that survived a revolution and was then left for a thousand years, gone black from soot, fire and decay, not painted black. Chunks are blasted out of the brick, bullet pockmarks come in clusters, and scorch blooms sit where fires burned. Bricks are charred greys of uneven size and tone, some burnt warm, with chipped corners, pits and cracks. Mortar crumbles out into deep black gaps, grime streaks run down, and large soot patches keep the 480 × 320 tile from reading as a pattern. Use the `.nl-wall` class.
- **Their propaganda is falling.** Big, burned, bullet-holed corporate posters ("TRUST THE FEED", "ALWAYS CONNECTED. ALWAYS CARED FOR.") hang on the wall, sway, peel at a corner and drop away, then get pasted up again to fail again. They are generic fictional slogans, faded, always behind the neon, and still under reduced motion.
- **The wall has history.** Faded on top of the brick are:
  - war-torn company propaganda posters that survived the fighting: burned at the edges, shot through, scorched, some half ripped away, with the revolution's red sprayed over them: "SHARE MORE. WORRY LESS." (slashed), "OPT IN TO HAPPINESS" ("lies" sprayed across it), "THE CLOUD REMEMBERS FOR YOU" (half torn off), "SMILE. YOU'RE SYNCED." (X'd out);
  - older torn propaganda: "AGREE TO CONTINUE" (X'd in old red), "WE CARE ABOUT YOUR PRIVACY *shared with 1,412 partners", "CONVENIENCE IS FREEDOM" half buffed out with "is a leash" written over it;
  - buffed-out tags of past revolutions: "we were here", "no one is mean", "THEY SOLD THE LAST ONE TOO", "unplug", "read the terms", a half-gone SEVER stencil, an old ∞, tally marks.

  It is all grey and faded, so it never competes with the neon. Use only fictional, generic corporate messaging: never a real company's name, logo or slogan. Keep history marks clear of the wordmark; reframe the layer with `background-position` (the Cover does).
- **Ink: thick neon spray.** We are the revolution painted over the old wall. Our tags are thick, grainy neon strokes with soft overspray, a neon glow and a pulsing dark halo (see the Wordmark component). No sparkles or twinkling stars: that is the opposite of edgy. Blue on black is the core: `neon-cyan`, `neon-blue` and `nousloop-teal`. Use `neon-red` only for the cut: an X through "our data", a strike-through, the slash in "severance/".
- **Fresh paint.** Live paint (`nl-live`) drifts between the blues over 24s, because the change is the message. No drips: a drip only goes on when it can look like real paint, never as a decorative gimmick.
- **Pop.** A dark shadow (`nl-spray-shadow`, and `nl-tag-text` for slogans) pulses around every sprayed stroke every 3.2s, so the neon lifts off the wall.
- **Wordmark: built like the owl.** "NoUs L∞P" is constructed from neon tubes with riveted end plates and sprocket joints. The o is the owl's lens eye, the P is a pressure gauge venting neon steam, and the ∞ is in a constant struggle: it tears apart with a red spark and fixes itself, over and over. Use the Wordmark SVG, never retype it.
- **Slogan type: the `tag` family.** This is Sedgwick Ave, a thin hand-tagged graffiti face, with Permanent Marker as fallback. It holds until NoUs has its own full font. Sizes are `tag-slogan` and `tag-small`. Never set body copy in it, and never use it below 22px.
- **The loop:** a long, low figure 8 on its side, more than twice as wide as it is tall, sprayed in one continuous pass starting from the crossing. The SprayLoader shows that motion over and over.
- **Imagery:** night scenes, hooded figures, neon ∞ projections, red-X billboards. See the *Brand Identity* asset group (the rebellion room, "Let's sever humans", the Severs Pack wallpapers).
- **Mascot: the mechanical owl of Athena.** The little owl (*Athene noctua*) was the sacred bird of Athena, Greek goddess of wisdom and *strategic* war. It was stamped on Athenian coins and later known as the owl of Minerva. (It is not Hermes's: Hermes is the messenger, with winged sandals and the caduceus.) Ours is a machine that holds all the knowledge: eyes made of the ∞ (a lens in each lobe), a clockwork gear heart, plated wings, a crown of lit knowledge nodes and steel talons gripping the ∞. It sees in the dark, doesn't panic, and wins with the mind. It is never cute. Being a machine, it changes expression mechanically, through jointed neon parts and sprockets: **watching**, **severing** (while NoUs works), **alert** (data exposed) and **calm** (severed, safe). See the **Owl** component.

### 2. Sovereign: the product

Used for: the wonderland desktop, the sidecar, settings and dashboards. It is a dark HUD on `surface-deep` / `surface-mid`, and the components below are built for it.

- `emerald` is life: OK, connected, primary action, the λ prompt, the user's voice.
- `amber` is the Sentinel: warnings, in-progress, system voice, high neural load.
- `cyan` is the senses: mic and vision activity.
- `danger` is failure.
- One hairline, `border`; cards are `surface-raised` and never get a left accent rule.
- Type: `sans` (Inter) for titles and values; `mono` (JetBrains Mono) for labels, inputs, streams and buttons.

The **Clinical** theme is the light version from the brand ledger (white / `surface-mid` #f7fafc, `nous-slate` text) for documents, onboarding and the sidecar.

- **Lockup:** the two-colour ∞, with `loop-blue` (the machine) on the left lobe and `loop-yellow` (the human) on the right, beside the "nous loop / SOLUTIONS" wordmark in `wordmark-ink` on `paper`. Clear space around any ∞ equals the height of one lobe. Never outline it, rotate it, or set it in a colour outside the palette.

## Colour rules

- On brick, text is `neon-cyan` (15:1), `neon-blue` (6.3:1) or `ink`; `neon-red` (5.9:1) is for marks and short words only.
- In Sovereign, readable text uses `ink` or `ink-dim`. **`ink-faint` fails contrast in both themes** (2.6:1 / 4.0:1): use it only for placeholders and offline dots.
- `loop-blue` and `loop-yellow` are fills, never text on light grounds.
- State is never colour alone: badges carry a word, and vital cards say the state in their sub-line.

## Spacing, radius, motion

- Spacing steps are `space-2` to `space-40`. The common rhythms are an 8px gap between controls, 16px card padding, 24px rail padding and 32 × 40 page padding.
- Radii stay small and technical: 5px on controls (`radius-control`) and 8px on cards (`radius-card`). Pills and dots are the only round shapes.
- **Product motion** means life-signs only: 0.15s colour transitions, a 2s emerald pulse on connected dots, and a 3s amber "breathe" on the active node.
- **Street motion** means fresh paint: the dark shadow pulses every 3.2s, live colour drifts over 24s, and the loader sprays the loop every 2.8s.
- All motion stops under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
