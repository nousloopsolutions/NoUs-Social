# Emblem

The impossible pyramid: the surveillance pyramid with its all-seeing eye, turned upside down and rebuilt so it cannot exist, with the owl's lens where their eye used to be.

**The winks:**

- **Upside down:** the pyramid of power, inverted.
- **Impossible:** its three hollow neon bars weave over-under in a cycle, each passing over the next at its corner, an Escher structure that cannot be built. The system they sell as inevitable is impossible.
- **The eye is ours:** the all-seeing eye is replaced by the owl's lens, aperture breathing, pupil glowing. The watched watch back.

The line that goes with it is **"the system is impossible. we watch back/"**.

**Style:** hollow neon tubes (a dark core inside a glowing tube), riveted at the bars, with the same neon spill, dark halo, grainy ink and live colour (`nl-live`) as the Wordmark.

**Use it** for stickers, avatars, the favicon at large sizes, merch, and section breaks on the wall. Keep it at 120px or more.

**The consumer provides** an `aria-label` if the default doesn't fit. Copy the SVG from the preview.

Classes: `nl-emblem`, `nl-weave-cut`, `nl-tube-core`, plus the owl's lens classes and the spray layers.
