# NoUs Loop Solutions

We build calm, private tools for neurodivergent minds. They run on your own devices, and your data stays with you. We never sell it.

## What we're building

| Product | What it is | Status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Nousmoji** | A calm, expressive face that shows how your day is going. | In development |
| **NoNu-Note** | Private notes that help you remember what matters. Android first. | In development |
| **NoUs Loop desktop** | Your local helpers on one calm screen. | In development |
| **LASSY** | A local AI stack that sets itself up and stays out of the way. | Early |

## How we work

- **Local first.** Your data is processed on your device. Nothing leaves without you.
- **No data sales, ever.** No ads and no tracking. We don't train on your data.
- **Plain and calm.** Clear words, one next step, no pressure and no dark patterns.
- **Open formats.** Your data stays readable and portable, so you're never locked in.

Our products support everyday wellbeing and organization. They are not medical devices.
