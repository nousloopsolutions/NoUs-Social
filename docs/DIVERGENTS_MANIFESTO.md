# THE DIVERGENTS: Community Manifesto

*We're all some deviation from the mean.*

## Who Are We?

**Divergents** are the neurodivergent community co-creating Nous Loop—a social-emotional neuroprosthetic built BY us, FOR us.

We're called "Divergents" because:
- ✅ We own our neurodivergence (it's our identity, not our deficit)
- ✅ We diverge from neurotypical norms (that's a feature, not a bug)
- ✅ We build in public (radical transparency via DIVERGENCE_LEDGER)
- ✅ We augment, we don't "fix" (we're not broken)

## What We're Building

**Nous Loop: The SAID Engine**
- Social Awareness Insight Detection
- Real-time conversation coaching
- Situational awareness support
- Emotional regulation tools
- Built with privacy-first federated learning

## Community Values

1. **Radical Transparency**
   - We document our pivots in DIVERGENCE_LEDGER
   - Decisions are logged in COUNCIL_MINUTES
   - Code is open-source (except private data in Vault)

2. **Privacy as Sacred**
   - Your data never leaves your device (federated learning)
   - No surveillance, no selling data
   - Privacy Gravity (η ≥ 0.95) enforced by architecture

3. **Co-Creation, Not Consumption**
   - You're not a "user," you're a co-builder
   - Your experience shapes the roadmap
   - Community votes on feature priorities

4. **Neurodiversity as Strength**
   - Different processing = different insights
   - Your "disability" might be our design breakthrough
   - Accessibility is innovation
